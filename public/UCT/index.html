Challenge 2: the school vineyard approval path

1. SGB and principal

Minute approval for use of land and water, and that learners maintain the vineyard. Without this, WCED will not engage.

2. WCED district office

Schools are provincial land. Infrastructure and Facilities approve digging deeper than 500 mm, trellising, or changes to water use.

3. Other departments

Client lists Basic Education, Agriculture and Public Works. Trade, Industry and Competition may also be needed.

4. Outcome

One application denied. The other two have progressed at an unequal rate, which points to gaps in the process.

The denied application is the clearest sign of fragmented government process. The land was reserved for future school development.

Our recommendation

Run The Township Winery as two linked engines: a premium range that funds the business, and an impact range that carries the story. Manage both through one scorecard and a leadership plan that starts now.

  • 1. Formalise roles and leadership: shareholder agreement, written roles, one paid role, a production succession plan
  • 2. Tier the portfolio on purpose: Philippi for volume, events and gifting; single-vineyard for margin, export and CSI buyers
  • 3. Re-route the school path: SGB and principal first, then WCED district; start with the most advanced school; test a lower-permission first phase
  • 4. Run one scorecard for quality and impact, reviewed quarterly
  • 5. Diversify funding: Founders' Club, farm CSI, philanthropy; ring-fence school-vineyard money
