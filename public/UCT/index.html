Challenge 2: the school vineyard approval path

1

SGB and principal

Minute approval for use of land and water, and that learners maintain the vineyard. Without this, WCED will not engage.

2

WCED district office

Schools are provincial land. Infrastructure and Facilities approve digging deeper than 500 mm, trellising, or changes to water use.

3

Other departments

Client lists Basic Education, Agriculture and Public Works. Trade, Industry and Competition may also be needed.

4

Outcome

One application denied. The other two have progressed at an unequal rate, which points to gaps in the process.

The denied application is the clearest sign of fragmented government process. The land was reserved for future school development.

Leadership succession: the priority

Priority 1

What we heard

  • Production sits with one senior co-founder, with no named successor
  • No contingency plan if the production lead is unavailable
  • Four owners, split 38 / 26 / 26 / 10
  • 16 years run on unpaid effort
  • Leadership conversation set for next month

The risk

  • Losing or sidelining the production lead halts the wine and the business
  • Decisions rest on the same few people
  • No agreed buy-out or succession terms among owners

What we would set up

  • Production handover: documented methods, supplier and vineyard contracts
  • A trained second person in production
  • Named roles, decision rights and a monthly leadership meeting
  • Shareholder agreement covering exit, incapacity and succession
  • One paid role first

The gap on contingency planning came up in our group conversation with the client. It is not in the written thread.

Our recommendation

Lead with leadership. A succession and organisation plan is the most valuable thing we can give The Township Winery, because everything else depends on it.

  • 1Leadership succession: name a successor and a contingency for production, and document the know-how
  • 2Organisation: define roles, decision rights and owner terms; fund one paid role
  • 3Two ranges: the Philippi and single-vineyard ranges already exist. Give each a clear job: community and gifting, or premium and CSI
  • 4School path: triage the three schools, then start with the furthest along, with a phased first step
  • 5One scorecard for quality and impact, with funding spread across several sources
