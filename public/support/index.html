Need help with Final Dayz? Email dpbaglini@gmail.com and include:

  • your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version,
  • a short description of the problem, and
  • a screenshot if you can take one.

How do I play on iPhone?

Hold your left thumb on the left side of the screen to move. Hold your right thumb on the right side to fire. Tap Tools to open Melee, Mine, and Turret. Tap the weapon button to switch guns.

Aim assist is making my aim feel wrong. What do I do?

Turn it off from the start menu with Aim Assist: On/Off. While it is on, push the fire pad past about halfway for precise manual aim.

My high score didn't show up on the leaderboard.

The shared leaderboard needs an internet connection. The app saves the score on your device and tries to send it the next time it connects.

The leaderboard takes a while to load.

The shared leaderboard server can take up to a minute to wake up after a quiet period. Single Player works right away.

How do I turn off sound?

Tap the ♪ button in the top corner, or use the Sound button in the shop.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. This removes the scores and settings stored on your device. Scores already sent to the shared leaderboard can be removed on request. Email dpbaglini@gmail.com.
