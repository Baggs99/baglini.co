Privacy Policy for Final Dayz

Effective date: October 20, 2026
Developer: Dan Baglini
Contact: dpbaglini@gmail.com

Final Dayz is a top-down zombie defense game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what information the app stores and sends.

Information the app does not collect

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, or device identifiers are collected by the app.
  • No advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs are included in the app.
  • The app does not track you across other apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

The app saves these items locally on your device, using the app's own storage:

  • Local high scores: the top scores on your device, with the initials you entered.
  • Last initials: the initials you used last, so they are filled in next time.
  • Personal best: your best run on this device.
  • Settings: whether sound is muted, whether touch aim assist is on, and whether the first-run touch tutorial has been shown.

This data stays on your device. Deleting the app removes it.

Information sent to our servers

The shared leaderboard uses a server operated by the developer. When you save a high score and have an internet connection, the app sends:

  • the initials you entered (up to three characters), and
  • your score.

The server stores the shared top scores so they appear on everyone's leaderboard. Your score and initials are the only game data sent.

When the app connects to the server, the hosting provider may keep standard server logs, which can include your IP address and the time of the request. These logs are not used to identify you.

If you do not want to share a score, do not save initials at the end of a run. The game still works without an internet connection.

Children

Final Dayz is not directed at children under 13, and the app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated and the effective date will change. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the updated policy.
