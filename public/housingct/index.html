About this clinic

Interdisciplinary clinic across Architecture, Law, Management, and Environment, organized by the Yale Urban Design Workshop. Teams work with local nonprofits on development proposals and designs anchored by affordable housing. Partner: Connecticut Department of Housing. Proposals may be eligible for state funding for pilots or predevelopment.

This year the clinic is focused on small multifamily buildings of 2 to 6 units, missing middle housing, with the New Haven Land Bank, on infill sites, using prefab, modular, and CLT construction. Students work in three multidisciplinary teams. Official start is Friday, 11 September 2026. Week 0 is intro meetings by school; each school has a different first meeting.
